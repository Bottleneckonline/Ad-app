Block ads in your apps and games

AdFree stops the banners, pop-up videos and "watch an ad" screens that game and app ad networks load, for every app on your phone at once. It works on your phone, without an account, and without sending your browsing anywhere.

How it works

Before an app shows an ad, it has to look up the address of the ad server. AdFree runs a tiny filter on your phone that answers those lookups with "nothing here", so the ad never loads. All other traffic goes over your normal connection, untouched.

What it can't do

No app can block ads that come from the same servers as the content. That includes YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Twitch ads, and ads that are built into a game itself. Some games hold back rewards when their ads can't load; add the game's ad domain to AdFree's allowlist if you want those rewards.
