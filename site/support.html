Ads still show in YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or Facebook

These apps serve ads from the same servers as their videos and posts, so no DNS-based blocker can remove them without breaking the app. AdFree is built for ads from ad networks, which is how most games and other apps show ads.

A game stopped giving rewards, or an app won't load

Some apps refuse to work when their ads can't load. Open AdFree, look at "Recently blocked" while you use that app, and add the domain to the allowlist. You can also switch AdFree off for a moment.

Android: ads get through

Go to Settings → Network → Private DNS and choose Automatic or Off. A specific Private DNS provider sends lookups around AdFree. AdFree shows a warning when this is the case.

Tip: Settings → Network → VPN → AdFree → Always-on VPN keeps AdFree running after a restart.

Can I use another VPN at the same time?

No. Android and iOS allow only one VPN at a time, and AdFree uses the VPN slot to filter ads on your phone.

Does AdFree see my browsing?

No. Everything happens on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

Restoring your purchase

iPhone/iPad: open AdFree and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Android: enter the license key from your purchase email again. Lost it? Look up your order at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders, or email us.
