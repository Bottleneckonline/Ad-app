Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

AdFree ("the app") is made by PUBLISHER_NAME ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use AdFree on Android or iOS.

In short: AdFree does not collect, store, sell, share or use your personal data or your browsing activity. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

How AdFree handles your internet traffic

AdFree uses your phone's VPN feature (Android VpnService, iOS Network Extension) to run a filter on your device. Only DNS lookups (the step where an app asks for the address of a website or server) go through this filter. Your other traffic does not pass through AdFree at all.

  • Lookups for ad and tracker domains are answered on your phone and go no further.
  • All other lookups are sent, as they would be without AdFree, to a public DNS resolver: Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) or Quad9 (9.9.9.9). Those services see the domain name being looked up and your IP address, under their own privacy policies (Cloudflare, Quad9). We do not receive this information.
  • AdFree keeps a short list of recently blocked domain names and simple counters so it can show them to you. These stay on your phone, are kept in memory only, and are cleared when the filter stops.
  • AdFree does not run any server that your traffic passes through.

Because AdFree offers VPN functionality, we commit that we do not sell, use or disclose to third parties any data about you or your traffic for any purpose.

Settings stored on your phone

Your allowlist (domains you chose never to block), the date you first opened the app (for the free trial) and whether you have unlocked the app are stored only on your phone.

Purchases

iPhone and iPad: purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have bought the unlock.

Android: AdFree for Android is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which processes the payment and emails you a license key. Lemon Squeezy handles your name, email address and payment details under its privacy policy; we can see your order (name, email, country, amount) in order to give support and refunds. When you activate a key, the app sends the key and your phone's model name (for example "Samsung SM-S911B") to Lemon Squeezy's license server, and checks the key again about once a week. Nothing else is sent.

Children

AdFree does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
